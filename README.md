Findings & Recommendations — Week 4 Capstone

 Key Findings:

Government and Small Business are the top-performing segments by total sales (₹118.73M overall), while Midmarket and Channel Partners contribute very little.
Enterprise sells a lot but is the least profitable segment — despite ranking 3rd in sales, it has the lowest profit of all segments. This suggests high costs or heavy discounting on Enterprise deals.
Sale Price is the strongest driver of sales (regression coefficient: 1183), far more influential than Units Sold or Discounts.
Discounts have almost no positive effect on profit — correlation is weak (0.38), and the scatter plot shows profit actually drops as discounts increase past a certain point.
The regression model explains 84% of the variation in sales (R² = 0.84), indicating a strong, reliable fit.

Recommendations:

Investigate Enterprise segment costs — since it sells well but earns little profit, review pricing or cost structure specific to Enterprise deals.
Reduce reliance on discounting — data shows discounts don't meaningfully boost profit and often hurt it; a more selective discount strategy is advised.
Prioritize Government and Small Business segments for growth investment, as they show the strongest sales-to-profit performance.
Use the regression model to forecast expected sales for new pricing scenarios before finalizing deals.
